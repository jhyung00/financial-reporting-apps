Accounting Impact (Non-Cash)

By extending the remaining life to 5.5 years, Meta reduces its annual depreciation expense to $22.2B. This accounting change significantly boosts reported Net Income, even though it generates zero actual cash.

Cash Flow Reality

The fleet is physically 32% used up. Regardless of the paper lifespan management chooses, the hardware is degrading. A high % used up signals an impending CapEx cliff—a massive real cash outflow will soon be required to replace these dying servers.

The Golden Rule of Intangibles

Internally generated intangibles (like building the Mickey Mouse brand) are almost always expensed immediately because determining their exact cost and lifespan is subjective.

Purchased intangibles (like acquiring Pixar or Marvel) have a clear market price. They are capitalized onto the balance sheet.
