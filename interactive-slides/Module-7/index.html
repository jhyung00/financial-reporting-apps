The Liability Spectrum

Liabilities are claims by non-owners on the assets of the company.

1. Current Liabilities

< 1 Year Maturity

  • Accrued Liabilities: Wages, Interest, Taxes.
  • Deferred Revenue: Cash received, work not done.

Verizon Case Study

Series 2030 Notes: 4.25% Coupon. Priced at 99.528%.

Question

Why is the price 99.528% (Discount)?

Answer

The Coupon (4.25%) was LOWER than the Market Rate (Yield) investors demanded (4.37%). To sell the bond, they had to lower the price.

Executive Summary

Short-Term Debt

  • Accruals: Essential for matching. Without them, NI is distorted.
  • Warranties: High risk of "Cookie Jar" manipulation. Analysts smooth this using historical rates.

Long-Term Debt

  • Pricing: PV of Cash Flows. Inverse relationship between Rate and Price.
  • Discount: Coupon < Market.
  • Premium: Coupon > Market.
  • Interest Expense: Driven by Effective Rate, not cash paid.
  • Maturity Wall: Critical for assessing liquidity risk.
